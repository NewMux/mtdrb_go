Privacy policy

Effective {{.Effective}}

CoachPulse is software that independent personal trainers use to run their business: their clients, sessions, training programmes, invoices and books. It is operated by {{.Entity}}, {{.Address}} (“we”). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Two kinds of people, two roles

Trainers hold a CoachPulse account. For their account details we decide what is collected and why: we are the controller.

Their clients do not need an account. A trainer records details about the people they train. For that information the trainer is the controller and we process it on their behalf and on their instructions: we are the processor. If you are a client, your trainer is the first person to ask about your information; we will help them answer you.

What we collect

From trainers:

  • Account details: name, email address, a password (stored only as a one-way hash), and, if you turn it on, a two-step sign-in secret (stored encrypted).
  • Business details: the practice’s name, country, time zone, currency, tax registration number, VAT settings, locations and price list, and the payment instructions you choose to put on invoices (for example a bank account number).
  • The devices you sign in from, as a list of sessions you can see and end from Settings.

What trainers record about their clients:

  • Contact details, tags and notes.
  • Health information the trainer collects for safe training: the PAR-Q questionnaire, signed waivers, measurements, and emergency medical notes. Medical notes are encrypted in the database, separately from everything else.
  • Progress photos and other files, kept in private storage and only ever reachable through links that expire within minutes.
  • Sessions, attendance, training programmes and the sets and loads logged in them.
  • Invoices and the payments the trainer records as received. CoachPulse does not take card payments and never sees card details.

Operational data: server logs of requests (the address and time, never the content of medical notes or payment details, which are removed before anything is written), and error reports used to fix faults.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service a trainer signed up for, which is the legal basis for most of it: performing our contract with the trainer.
  • To keep accounts secure: rate limits on sign-in, two-step sign-in, revoking sessions.
  • To email what the service needs to send, such as a password-reset link. We do not send marketing email without asking.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping a trainer’s tax records intact while their account exists.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use client health information for anything other than providing the service to the trainer who recorded it.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers, under contract and only for running CoachPulse: a hosting provider for the servers and database, an object-storage provider for files, an email provider for account emails, and an error-reporting service. Each practice’s records are kept apart from every other practice’s by the database itself, not only by our code.

How long we keep it

  • For as long as the trainer’s account exists.
  • When a trainer deletes their account in the app, nobody can sign in to it from that moment, and everything the practice holds — its clients, records, files and books — is permanently deleted {{.PurgeDays}} days later. The delay exists so a deletion made by mistake can be undone; write to us within it if that happens.
  • Backups roll over and are gone within 35 days of a deletion.
  • Expired sign-in sessions and reset links are deleted automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to complain to a data protection authority. Trainers can correct their details and delete their account from Settings in the app. Clients should contact their trainer first. Anyone may write to us at {{.ContactEmail}}; we answer within 30 days.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Medical notes and two-step secrets are encrypted at rest. Each practice’s records are isolated by row-level security in the database, and files are private. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

CoachPulse accounts are for adults running a business. A trainer who trains minors is responsible for having the consent of a parent or guardian for what they record.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top is when the current version started.
